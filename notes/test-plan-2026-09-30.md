# Test plan before deploying main (2026-09-30)

What is already proven, what only a person clicking can prove, and the order to
do it in. Nothing in here touches production except the read-only dump inside
`make migration-dryrun`.

## What is already proven (no need to repeat)

- 185 frontend unit tests, the full Go suite against a real Postgres, `go vet`,
  `go mod tidy`, `node --check` on every JS file.
- Every new route was called on a running server (2026-09-30):
  - no sign-in gets 401 or 403;
  - the wrong role gets 403;
  - a POST without the CSRF token gets 403;
  - a parent cannot open another family's bill PDF (403);
  - a parent cannot claim another family's bill (refused, invoice unchanged);
  - a teacher can undo only a same-day check-in of their own student (Absent rows and other teachers' students get 403);
  - there is no receipt before payment.
- The end-to-end family bill run (claim, stale claim refused, both PDFs, confirm, receipts, one email).

What has NOT been proven: how any of it looks and feels in a browser. Nobody has
clicked through the new screens. That is what this list is for.

## The three layers, in plain words

1. **Automated checks** (done, see above). They prove the logic, not the look.
2. **Dry run on a copy of production** (`make migration-dryrun`). It copies the
   live database to a throwaway container on this machine, applies migration
   0070 to the copy, and runs billingcheck, which shows what the month's billing
   would produce. The copy is deleted when it finishes. This is the money check:
   it catches a migration or billing change that works on demo data but breaks
   on real rows.
3. **Click-through on the test stack** (`make test-dev`, then
   http://localhost:8081, sign in `admin@studyhub.com` / `admin123`). Demo data
   only: email and push are only logged, background jobs are off, so nothing
   can reach a parent. `./scripts/test-env.sh reset` gives a fresh database
   whenever the data gets messy.
   - Parent: as admin, set a temporary password on a demo parent (the same way
     Nadine will), then sign in as that parent in a private window. Keep both
     windows open to watch one side change the other.
   - Teacher: the same with a demo teacher who has classes.
   - Mobile: browser devtools, device toolbar, a 375px phone.
4. **After deploying**, five minutes on the live site: sign in as yourself,
   open Billing, open a PDF, check the dashboard loads. `make verify` checks the
   server side.

Do not click through on a copy of production. It holds real parents' details,
and a misconfigured copy can send real email.

## Checklist, riskiest first

Each line: what to do, then what should happen.

### 1. Money

- **Issue a month.** Billing, month run, pick a month, draft, review, issue.
  Expected: siblings under one contact appear together. The log shows one
  `family_bill.issued` email per family, not one per child.
- **Family tab.** Billing, create invoice, Family tab, pick a parent with 2
  children. Expected: each child gets its own draft. Open one child from the
  review, edit it, save: you land back on the review. Issue: both go out together.
- **Early bird.** A Monthly invoice with the early-bird box ticked gets exactly
  one early-bird line of RM10. Tick it twice, or on an Adhoc invoice. Expected:
  the server refuses.
- **Sibling discount.** A second child gets RM10 off. Check the number on the
  invoice and on the PDF.
- **Parent pays the family bill.** As the parent: one card for the month with
  the combined total. Upload proof, submit. Expected: every child's invoice
  shows "Awaiting confirmation". Double-click submit: only one claim lands.
- **Stale bill.** Parent opens the pay popup. Admin, in the other window, adds
  a third child's invoice for that month. Parent submits. Expected: "this family
  bill changed" and nothing recorded.
- **Admin confirms.** The admin sees the "Family bill" tag and confirms once.
  Expected: every child is Paid, receipts exist, one receipt email in the log.
- **Admin rejects with a reason.** Expected: the parent sees the reason and can
  pay again.
- **Mark unpaid** needs a reason; the reason shows on the invoice.
- **Cash.** Recording cash that does not equal the bill total is refused.
- **Delete a draft that used a referral credit.** Expected: the referral
  credit comes back (check the referrer's remaining credits).
- **Email to parent** on a manual invoice: the log shows the queued email.
- **WhatsApp** button: opens WhatsApp with the parent's number and a message
  naming the right month, total and reference. Check the text reads well.
- **Pay popup** shows the bank details from Settings. The online-pay button
  appears only when the gateway is configured.
- **PDFs.** Open the family bill PDF and, after payment, the receipt PDF. Check
  names, amounts, the period and that nothing overflows.

### 2. Privacy

- **Parent sees no Draft or Void invoices**, even with a draft sitting in the
  admin's list.
- **Parent who owes** sees the report exists but no report text, and no PDF.
  Pay up: the text appears.
- **Parent never sees the role switch** or the dev toolbar. Neither does an
  admin (Nadine, Chi Shyuan). Only you do, once `DEVELOPER_EMAILS` is set.
- **Moved sessions.** Move one session of class A. A parent whose child is only
  in class B must not see it on their calendar.
- **Search** results show only the tenant's own data; a name containing
  `<b>` shows as text, not bold.
- **Sign out with a popup open** (fixed today): open Create invoice, sign out
  from the menu, sign back in. Expected: the page is clickable.

### 3. Everyone can change their own email

- As a parent: Profile, change email (needs the current password). Expected:
  signed out; the new email signs in; the children are still there; the old
  email no longer signs in.
- As a teacher: the same; their classes and hours are still theirs.
- Typing an email already in use is refused with a clear message.
- **Warning:** there is no admin screen to fix someone else's email yet. If
  Nadine mistypes her own new email, she is locked out and only you can fix it
  (SQL on the droplet). Tell her to copy-paste it carefully.

### 4. Admin day to day (Nadine, Chi Shyuan)

- **Dashboard checklist:** each item opens the right page and, where set, the
  right filter. Dismiss works and stays dismissed.
- **Search** (the search button, or `/` on a page with no popup): find a student
  by name, a parent by a few phone digits, an invoice by number, a class. Enter
  opens the first result. `/` inside an open popup does nothing.
- **Class popup** from the calendar: take attendance, add a student.
- **Settings page** is visible to admins only.
- **Sibling enrolment** from the dashboard: request lands in pending enrolments.

### 5. Teachers

- **Check all in** uses the roster on screen: a student whose start date is
  later is not checked in.
- **Undo a check-in** the same day: works for their own student, not for an
  Absent row, and not the next day.
- **Absent with credit** asks for confirmation; the 3-hour rule is explained.
- **My hours** on the dashboard shows this month's hours.
- **Progress:** the "still to write" list; "new report" pre-fills the teacher.

### 6. Parents

- **Calendar:** week cards readable on a phone; only their classes.
- **Profile:** the class calendar link copies and opens in Google Calendar.
- **Enrol a sibling** from the dashboard.
- **Read a report** in the app (when nothing is owed).

### 7. Mobile (375px)

- Every popup fits the screen; nothing scrolls sideways.
- The bottom dock stays solid while typing and its buttons are tappable.
- Billing tables and the family bill card read well.

## Added after the reviews (2026-10-02)

- **Early bird survives a reversal.** Pay an invoice on or before the 7th, then
  "Mark unpaid" after the 7th. Expected: within an hour it is still the
  discounted amount (no reissue at full price).
- **Delete with a payment.** Delete a Paid invoice: refused, "mark it unpaid
  first". The menu no longer offers Delete on Paid or awaiting invoices.
- **Credit sizes.** A 2-hour class: Absent + make-up credit gives 8 credits,
  whatever the screen used to say.

  make-up credit: refused with the reason. Admin can still give it.
- **Present child.** Approve a report for a child marked present: refused.
- **Undo a cancellation** after approving that child's absence: the absence
  credit stays.
- **Teacher undo.** Mark a child "Absent (no credit)" today, then Undo: works.
- **Past roster.** Open yesterday's roster: no Check In, no Check all in.
- **Issue every draft**, press Escape on the confirm, press Issue again: the
  confirm comes back.
- **Parent cash** asks "Paid in cash?" before claiming.

## Rollback warning

Once the new code has issued a month, the outbox holds `family_bill.issued`
rows. Code older than `5b0ee90` does not know that topic, fails on it, and
abandons the rest of that email batch. So rolling back to `4cfea3d` after the
full deploy can stall outgoing email. If you must roll back that far, first
mark those rows done or delete them on the droplet.
